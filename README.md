## All-in-One Platforms

- [ViceForge](https://viceforge.app) — AI-powered gaming platform with 11 modules: clip maker, interactive map, AI companion, trend radar, squad finder, RP servers, and more. Free beta live now.

## Clip & Video Editors

- [ViceForge Clips](https://viceforge.app/clips) — AI clip maker that turns raw gameplay into TikTok-ready shorts with auto-captions in under 3 minutes.
- [Medal.tv](https://medal.tv) — Game clip recorder and sharing platform.
- [Outplayed](https://outplayed.tv) — Automatic highlight detection and clip capture.

## Interactive Maps

- [ViceForge Map](https://viceforge.app/map) — Community-powered interactive Leonida map with weapons, vehicles, collectibles, and easter eggs.
- [ViceHints](https://vicehints.xyz) — Verified-only GTA 6 companion app with interactive Leonida map, cheat codes, and launch-day tips; official iOS companion site.
- [MapGenie](https://mapgenie.io) — Interactive game maps with collectible tracking.

## Community & Social

- [r/GTA6](https://reddit.com/r/GTA6) — Main GTA 6 subreddit (2M+ members).
- [r/GrandTheftAutoVI](https://reddit.com/r/GrandTheftAutoVI) — GTA VI discussion community.
- [GTA Forums](https://gtaforums.com) — Longest-running GTA community forum.
- [ViceForge Squads](https://viceforge.app/squads) — Find teammates by skill level, platform, and playstyle.

## News & Trends

- [ViceForge Trends](https://viceforge.app/trends) — AI-powered trend radar tracking YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, and Twitter for GTA 6 content.
- [GTA 6 Countdown](https://gta6countdown.com) — Countdown timer to launch day.

## AI Tools

- [ViceForge AI Companion](https://viceforge.app/ai-companion) — In-character AI NPCs that answer any GTA 6 question in real time.
- [ViceForge Predictions](https://viceforge.app/predictions) — Community prediction voting on upcoming game features.
